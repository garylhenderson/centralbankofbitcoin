Your next step

Only open your vault here. Burning the NFT any other way (a wallet's burn button, another app) destroys everything inside it forever.

This page checks the vault for anything that would be destroyed and tells you before you sign. Nothing is signed until you review and confirm the action.

What happens next

  1. Choose your vault

    Select a vault held in your Gary Club wallet.

  2. Inspect what comes out

    Review the reserves, account setup, network fees, and any assets at risk.

  3. Confirm the opening

    Your vault is destroyed permanently. Its recoverable reserves arrive in your wallet.
