Your next step

What happens next

  1. Check the round

    New vaults are available only while a sale round is open.

  2. Review the price

    Your payment goes to the Bank’s revenue address: 85% to participating vaults and 15% to the treasury. The holder-approved price is at least 21,000 sats. Your new vault starts empty; the purchase price is not its activation deposit. A small SOL balance pays the network and creates the on-chain accounts.

  3. Make it yours

    The level is assigned on chain. Your new vault appears in your Gary Club wallet.

  4. Activate participation

    After its level is drawn, deposit at least 21,000 sats of cbBTC into the vault and request activation. It joins after a distribution and earns from the following one. The deposit alone does not start payouts.

    The founding cohort can first join the payout pool no earlier than thirty days after charter close. Its first automatic payout is no earlier than sixty days after close. After joining, a payout triggered by burning 100 $GARY can arrive sooner.
