How this record works
01

One vault, one vote.

The owner votes. A transfer does not reset a vault’s vote. Gary Club holds its custodial wallets’ keys and can sign votes for them.

02

A fixed electorate.

Vaults issued after a proposal opens cannot vote on it.

03

Decisions have limits.

A vote cannot take a vault’s reserves, change the royalty, or raise the 2,100 supply cap.
