Your next step

What happens next

  1. Choose your proof

    Sign in to see eligible Proofs of Burn in your Gary Club wallet.

  2. Review the exchange

    The Proof of Burn is destroyed. The charter vault is issued at no cost. A small SOL balance pays the network and creates the on-chain accounts.

  3. Receive your vault

    The level is assigned on chain. Follow its reserves from your desk.

  4. Activate participation

    After its level is drawn, deposit at least 21,000 sats of cbBTC into the vault, then request activation. The vault joins after a distribution and earns from the following one. The founding cohort’s first joining tick is no earlier than thirty days after charter close. Holding an unactivated vault earns no payouts.
