The alarm detects a change after the fact; it cannot judge whether a new Core or DLMM build is benign, and our immutable programs cannot adapt. 5. **Test the holder pages with a real holder and a real wallet** (item 50): that a Phantom user completes a redemption, understands the tick-boxes, and that the `/rpc` proxy and the metadata generator hold up.
