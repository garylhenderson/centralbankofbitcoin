## R2 / R2b — The pool and the boost

I recomputed the invariant "the pool holds at least ceil(owed)": `credit` adds `delta*w ≤ avail*ACC_SCALE` to `owed`; `claim` pays `floor(pending/ACC)` and decrements `owed` by exactly `pay*ACC`; `retire` subtracts the full pending (including the forfeit case); `reweigh` preserves each vault's pending exactly (carry = owed, debt = new weight × acc) and moves `total_active_weight` by the difference; `join` enters at `debt = w*acc`, carry 0. Sum of pendings tracks `owed_scaled` through every path I could construct, including the wrapped-ring retires (BO.38/38a) and the frozen-destination expiry (BO.37/37a). `distribute` refuses `PoolShort` and `NothingToDistribute` before crediting; `monthly_inline` uses `saturating_sub` and treats a non-token pool as empty.

Counters under every ordering: `pending_this_epoch` is zeroed only in `advance_epoch`; joins require `dist_epoch > request_epoch` so they only remove earlier-epoch requests; retires decrement both counters only for same-epoch requests (F21.1). `waiting_to_join = pending_count - pending_this_epoch` is exact. Liveness: every gate (`JoinWaitingFirst`, `BoostsExpireFirst`, a short pool, a frozen pool) is clearable by a permissionless, free instruction, and the crank does so; I found no ordering that makes a distribution or a redemption permanently impossible for others. One noted asymmetry, not a defect: `join` indexes `LEVEL_WEIGHTS[level-1]` without checking the level, but every path to `pending` requires `activate`'s `NotRevealed` check, so pending ⇒ level ≥ 1.

The boost: a boost earns exactly N distributions (the same-transaction distribution counts, BO.21; a preceding one does not, BO.35); `advance_epoch` drains slot `dist_epoch % 64` into `boosts_due` the moment the end epoch is reached, and `BoostsExpireFirst` prevents any distribution from running past a due boost, so `unschedule_boost`'s two branches are mutually exclusive and exact; `epochs ≤ 63` guarantees the end slot never collides with the buy epoch's slot; expiry is idempotent (weight 0 → `BoostNotDue`); the ring, totals and due counts are modelled across four wraps by the host test and recomputed from records by the suite's `books()`/`ledger()` after every money operation. Nobody can make distributions impossible by boosting (expiring is free and permissionless) or by a vanished token account (`expire_boosts` needs only records; `claim` expires too).
