## Holder guide amendments for approval

- Accounts at the Bank are held through Gary Club, the parent organization.
  Gary Club holds the key to the account's wallet and signs what the member
  approves. Members who use their own wallet hold their own key.
- A charter claim has no vault purchase price. SOL pays network and account
  creation costs. An issued vault starts empty. Activation needs at least
  21,000 sats of cbBTC deposited into the vault, followed by an activation
  request. A level draw alone does not activate payouts.
- Proposed trading terms (D7/D13): trades settle in SOL; the seller receives
  90%, and 10% goes to the Bank's revenue address. That revenue follows the
  fixed 85/15 split. There is no additional Gary Club fee. Standing listings
  and offers authorize Gary Club to settle automatically at the stated price;
  they do not ask for another signature at acceptance. Offers reserve no funds.
- Open a vault only through the Bank's opening procedure. Opening permanently
  destroys the vault and releases recoverable reserves. Another kind of burn
  can strand the reserves. Openings are limited to 2.1% of live vaults per
  rolling hour, rounded up.
- Metaplex Core is an external dependency. Locking the Bank's programs does not
  lock Core. Before the Bank's programs are locked, the exact live Core build
  must be public, verified, diffed and tested under Gary's D5(b) rule.
