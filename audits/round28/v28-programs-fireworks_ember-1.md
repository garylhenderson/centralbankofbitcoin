## R2 / R2b. THE POOL AND THE BOOST — the invariant holds

I traced every mutation of `owed_scaled`, `total_active_weight`, `pending_count`, `pending_this_epoch`, `boosts_active`, `boosts_due`, `boost_weight_total` and the ring:

- **Pool ≥ ceil(owed).** `credit` adds `delta*w ≤ avail*SCALE` to owed with the balance unchanged; `claim` subtracts exactly `pay*SCALE` as it pays `pay`; `join` owes nothing; `reweigh` moves owed into `carry` and restarts debt at the new weight (owed unchanged); `retire` (redeemed) requires `pending < SCALE` and leaves the sub-sat residue in the pool; the forfeit and bare-burn paths subtract all of `pending` while the sats stay in the pool (surplus). `PoolShort` is unreachable defensive code. P.32 and the `ledger()`/`books()` checks (BK.IN/BK.3c/BK.P, which record a label only if the books balanced after each named money operation) cover this on chain.
- **Counters.** `advance_epoch` zeroes `pending_this_epoch`; `waiting_to_join = pending_count − pending_this_epoch` is exactly "requested in an earlier epoch, unjoined"; a same-epoch retire decrements both, an earlier-epoch retire only `pending_count` (F21.1/F21.2). No underflow path found (`checked_sub` everywhere; overflow aborts, measured fact 6).
- **Liveness.** `JoinWaitingFirst` and `BoostsExpireFirst` are both clearable by anyone, free (`claim` joins, `expire_boosts` needs only records). A griefer must fund a vault with 21,000 sats per blocked epoch and anyone can clear it; the crank does it every pass. No permanent distribution or redemption block found.
- **Boost exactness.** A boost bought in epoch `e` with length `n` ends at `e+n`, earns exactly the distributions advancing `e→e+1 … e+n−1→e+n`, and is gated before any further distribution. BO.21/BO.35 pin both in-transaction orderings ([boost, distribute] counts the ride; [distribute, boost] does not). The ring cannot collide: `n ≤ 63 = BOOST_RING − 1`, a slot is drained before it can be reused, and the 260-epoch host test drives buys, wrapped buys, early/due retirements and expiries through the program's own functions with per-slot model checks and coverage minimums. `expire_boosts` skips (not errors) records whose boost is gone, so raced batches land; a malformed account refuses the call, which is caller error, not a grief vector (the caller chooses the accounts).
- **Terms.** `BoostTermsMoved` is one-sided exactly as ruled (BO.26/BO.30/BO.31 refuse adverse moves and burn nothing; BO.30a lands a favorable move at the new price). Owner-only is enforced against the asset's live Core owner bytes (BO.6, BO.33/BO.33a).
