## Chairman statement 2026-8: The charter window

Good afternoon. The Bank did not print today.

The founding charter window opens Monday, October 19, at noon Austin time.
It closes Monday, October 26, at noon Austin time. Those dates are now recorded
permanently on chain.

A Proof of Burn is presented during that window to claim its charter vault.
The charter vault is free. Network fees and account rent still apply.

The vault begins empty. Activation requires its own deposit of at least 21,000
satoshis in cbBTC. A claim is not a deposit. The distinction remains in force
regardless of how pleased the claimant is.

Activation does not begin payouts immediately. No vault joins the payout pool
earlier than 30 days after the charter closes. The first automatic payout is no
earlier than 60 days after close. After joining, a payout triggered by burning
100 $GARY may occur sooner, when new funds are available. A date does not
guarantee an amount.

Use the Bank's claim page. Sign in with your Gary Club account or connect the
wallet that holds your Proof of Burn. Gary Club holds the keys to Gary Club
wallets. The Bank does not need the recovery phrase of an external wallet.

Governors vote their own vaults. They may assign all eligible vaults to one side
or divide their vaults between Yes and No. A vault casts one vote per proposal.
Transferring it does not erase a vote already cast.

The program lock is targeted for Friday, October 16, at noon Austin time. It
requires a passing full test suite against Metaplex Core's exact live code in
the preceding 24 hours. If the checks fail, the programs will not be locked.
Metaplex Core remains an external dependency after the Bank's programs lock.

Read the holder guide before depositing. Open a vault only on the Bank's redeem
page. A wallet's burn button is not a withdrawal instruction.

The schedule has been entered. The checks remain required.

ROY E. MINTER, ACTING CHAIRMAN

## Holder notice

**Your founding vault: what to prepare**

The on-chain charter window runs from October 19 at noon through October 26 at
noon, Austin time. Those dates are permanent. Use
https://centralbankofbitcoin.com/claim/ and the account or
wallet holding your Proof of Burn. Gary Club sign-in is the default; standard
wallet connection is also available.

Your charter vault is free, apart from network fees and account rent. It starts
empty and needs its own deposit of at least 21,000 sats in cbBTC to activate.
Keep enough SOL in the signing wallet for the fees shown before you approve.

An activated vault cannot join payouts earlier than 30 days after charter close
(around November 25 for this schedule). The first automatic payout is no
earlier than 60 days after close (around December 25). After joining, a payout
triggered by burning 100 $GARY may arrive sooner if new funds are available.

Gary Club holds your Gary Club wallet's key. An external wallet remains under
your control. Never enter its recovery phrase on the Bank's website. Only use
https://centralbankofbitcoin.com/redeem/ to open a vault: opening destroys the
vault and returns its supported contents. The Bank limits openings to at most
2.1% of sealed vaults per hour, and Gary Club wallets require Gary Club's
signature. Read the holder guide linked from the claim page before depositing.

Claims and trading have separate release checks. This notice does not announce
that trading is available. The October 16 lock remains conditional on the fresh
full-suite check against live Metaplex Core. Check the claim page for the current
launch status.
