passing fixture is not evidence of a real member session or wallet signature.

## Before owner signing

- The owner opens the Bank in Brave and signs in with the existing Gary Club
  account. The return lands on the Bank, shows the expected Gary Club wallet and
  personal vault view, and has no `encode is not a function` error. Signing out
  removes private account/offer information. No password or session cookie enters
  logs or evidence. This is a real-browser check, not the intercepted fixtures.
- The external-wallet option connects the expected Phantom account and network.
  No phrase, key export or token transfer is requested just to connect.
- Public Market, governance and history work without login on phone and desktop.
  Before initialization, there is no invented sale round, price or earned payout.
- Both Gary capability switches are off in the environment and the running app.
  Signing delivery stays closed until the owner is present and the fresh delivery
  checks in SIGNING-DELIVERY-2026-10-12.md pass.

## Holder release

Real claims cannot be completed before the charter opens October 19 at noon
Austin time. Before that, finish real authentication/connection, fee and simulation
checks where chain state permits, plus the full local-validator tests. Do not
label a local simulation as a completed mainnet claim.

The owner must explicitly approve the supervised holder release window. During
that window use the actual holder's instructions and wallet; do not impersonate
a member or mint test vaults in mainnet outside the ruled protocol. Verify:

- One eligible Proof of Burn issues one vault. A second tab/reload cannot issue
  another for it. Cancelled reviews do nothing; uncertain responses recover the
  existing transaction before any retry. Save the actual chain receipt.
- Vault and wallet ownership agree in Gary Club, the Bank and independent chain
  reads. The new vault starts empty. Show the deposit address, deposit separately
  only with the holder's approval, and verify activation and payout timing.
- A whole-vault move discloses destination and contents, preserves votes and
  recovers a lost response. Verify each direction with its actual signing wallet.
- Opening review uses the Bank redeem page, includes fees and relevant warnings,
  and survives new deposits without silently expanding consent. Opening destroys
  a real vault; perform it only when that holder actually wants to open it.
- Governance assigns only the vaults/sides the holder approves, supports all or
  split assignments, shows prior votes and recovers partial completion. Its real
  transaction checks must respect the on-chain proposal window; do not fabricate
  a mainnet proposal or cast votes just to tick this checklist.

If a financial acceptance check fails, stop new actions via the holder switch,
preserve the existing signed receipt and use the recovery route. Do not replay
the action as if a timeout proved failure. This UI switch does not disable
direct on-chain instructions or change the permanent charter window.

## Separate trading release

Only after explicit approval of its supervised release window, verify seller
listing/cancellation, buyer offer, seller acceptance and listing purchase with
consenting real holders and the exact displayed price. Funds are not reserved
for an offer. Check the seller's 90%, the Bank revenue's 10%, and no extra Gary
Club fee against the actual successful settlement. Confirm vault ownership,
reserve contents, any already-cast votes, private notices and public sale history.

Verify an offer-arrival email with a consenting recipient: correct inbox and
vault, no duplicate on recovery, and opt-out respected. Provider acceptance alone
is not proof of inbox delivery. Financial and mail fixtures already cover races;
do not manufacture a paid sale or send unsolicited test mail for this checklist.

When changing the trading switch, synchronize the scoped public-data environment
described in JOB-ISOLATION.md, refresh market.json and compare its status with the
running Gary app. On failure, stop new trading while retaining recovery of
previously authorized transactions. Holder and trading controls stay separate.
